## 📐 Overview & Concept

This repository exists strictly as a visual design sample and portfolio piece. The design features a 3D isometric hexagonal emblem constructed from layered, gradient chevrons, paired with modern athletic brand typography and a motivational tagline stack.

* **Project Purpose:** Portfolio Piece / Brand Identity Concept
* **GitHub Upload Date:** August 10, 2026
* **Asset File:** `ATHELETICS_PORTFOLIO LOGO.png`
* **Format:** Square High-Resolution PNG Image

## 🔍 Design Breakdown

* **Typography & Tagline:**
  * **Brand Name:** `ATHLETICS` rendered in a bold, high-contrast, clean sans-serif typeface
  * **Tagline Stack:** `BE MORE / WANT MORE / DO MORE` arranged in three wide-tracked lines directly beneath the primary brand name
* **Visual Mark:**
  * Geometric 3D cube / hexagon icon composed of nested upward and downward chevron lines
  * Layered line depth creating a dimensional, isometric cube illusion
* **Color Palette:** Monochrome gradient transition (Black to Charcoal and Light Grey) for the icon, Black typography, and Electric Royal Blue for the tagline text on a Crisp White background
